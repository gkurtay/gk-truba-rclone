GK-TRUBA-RCLONE

GK-TRUBA-RCLONE is a personal, single-user OAuth client used by its owner to transfer, back up, restore, list, and verify the owner's own files between TRUBA high-performance computing systems and Google Drive using rclone.

Purpose

The application is used only for the owner's research-data management workflow. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide access to Google Drive data for third parties.

Data handling

File operations are initiated explicitly by the owner. Data is transferred directly between the owner's computing environment and the owner's Google Drive account through rclone.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details.

This site documents the OAuth client associated with the GK-TRUBA-RCLONE Google Cloud project.
